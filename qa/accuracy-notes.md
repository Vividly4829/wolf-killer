# Weapon accuracy rationale

The NPS explains that rifling spins a projectile and improves accuracy and range: https://www.nps.gov/fosc/learn/education/weaponevolution.htm
The NPS Brown Bess article describes the smoothbore and its limited accuracy: https://home.nps.gov/articles/brown-bess.htm
Royal Armouries contrasts Baker rifles with smoothbore muskets at Waterloo: https://royalarmouries.org/objects-and-stories/stories/waterloo-1815

These support relative ordering, not exact spread measurements for each catalog weapon. The game uses base angular half-deviations: smoothbore musket 0.025 radians; dueling flintlock 0.035; rifled longarms 0.0025–0.006. Short-barrel handguns are less precise; pellet weapons retain wider shot patterns. ADS, injury, movement and ritual modifiers still apply. The Lancaster's oval rifling is classified as rifled. Fantasy laser/flamethrower and thrown weapons are marked Special. UI degrees are a gameplay accuracy parameter, not a claim about historical proof-testing.
