# Angel arrival choir

angel_hallelujah.wav is a 10-second excerpt (6–16 seconds) of the Oratorio Chorus performing Handel's Hallelujah chorus, recorded May 1916 for Edison, matrix 4433-A-1-1 (6-1), NPS EDIS 43552.

Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Messiah_Hallelujah_Chorus_1916.ogg

The source identifies the recording as public domain. Adaptation: excerpt, peak normalization to 0.82, 100 ms fade-in and 1.2 s fade-out; PCM 16-bit WAV at 44.1 kHz. This is a historical sacred choral recording, not a claimed recording of a particular Catholic congregation.
