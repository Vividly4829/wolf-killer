# Audio

`opening_line.wav` is the original requested line, "Should go out and do something about these wolves.", synthesized locally with the installed Windows Microsoft David Desktop voice. Rebuild with `tools/build_voice.ps1`. It is synthetic speech, not a recording of a performer or audio from The Long Dark.

`wolf_howl_denali.mp3` is **Wolf**, a field recording from Denali National Park & Preserve, Alaska, credited to the **National Park Service**. The NPS source page identifies NPS-credited material without a copyright symbol as public domain; the Natural Sounds gallery also explicitly permits downloading its public-domain clips.

- Source and usage terms: https://www.nps.gov/media/video/view.htm?id=0F1BA318-917E-D79B-54FD1C9598BFD7BB
- Downloaded original: https://www.nps.gov/nps-audiovideo/legacy/mp3/nri/avElement/nri-WolfDENA.mp3
- Gallery usage statement: https://www.nps.gov/subjects/sound/gallery.htm
- Retrieved 19 September 2026. The bundled MP3 is unchanged. Playback uses positional attenuation, reduced gain and small per-animal pitch variations. Only one recording plays at a time, with a 14–21 second gap between starts, to avoid stacking the recorded answering calls into a constant chorus.
- SHA-256: `bfc073e6226f2a21a87567b898c5e5674a53284353128da0d1e0f69b97e8b62f`.

The 24 gunshot WAVs in `gunshots/` are original 48 kHz effects generated offline by `tools/build_gunshots.py`: muzzle crack, turbulent bass/midrange blast, pressure impulse and outdoor reflections. Each of eight firearm families has three variations. They contain no third-party recordings. Wolf growls and barks, wind, reload and other sound effects are generated by this project's `scripts/soundscape.gd`. The old synthesized howl has been removed. No audio from another game is included.

`flesh_hit_1.wav` through `flesh_hit_3.wav` are original generated impact Foley: broadband slap, low body thump and irregular wet crackles. Rebuild with `python tools/build_flesh_impacts.py` (Python standard library only). No third-party recordings are used.
