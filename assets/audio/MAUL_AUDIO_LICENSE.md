# Recorded maul audio

All recordings below are CC0; originals are retained in reference/audio.
- Canine snarls: qubodup, Dog Snarl Grunt Grumble, https://opengameart.org/content/dog-snarl-grunt-grumble (growl/snarl/grumble FLAC).
- Additional growl: bonebrah, https://opengameart.org/content/dog-growl .
- Human voices: HaelDB, https://opengameart.org/content/male-gruntyelling-sounds (yell1, 3yell16, 3yell13).
- Cloth ripping: Joseph Sardin, BigSoundBank sound 13, https://bigsoundbank.com/cloth-s0013.html .

Trimmed, normalized, mono 22.05 kHz PCM. Canine recordings serve as wolf sound design; they are not claimed to be wild-wolf recordings. Existing Denali wolf howls retained. No commercial-game recordings used.

Bear growl: U.S. Fish & Wildlife Service, distributed by AntumDeluge, CC0: https://opengameart.org/content/bear-growls . Normalized FLAC converted to PCM WAV. Source bear.zip retained.
